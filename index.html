What we do

📚

Book donation

Collect old textbooks, novels and exam guides, then pass them to students who can't afford new ones.

🌱

Sustainability series

One practical eco habit or green fact, every day. Small changes that cut waste at home.

🛠️

Tools & green tech

Free web tools for learning and reuse, and technology that saves energy or waste.

Give a book, give a chance

Have books you no longer need? Send them to us and we will match them with a student.

  1. Gather your old books. Textbooks, novels and guides are all useful.
  2. Message us on Facebook with your city and roughly how many books you have.
  3. Drop off or arrange a pickup. We'll confirm the details with you.

Need a book?

Tell us the subject and class. We'll do our best to find a copy near you.
